Welcome to Sol

A live, accurate model of the Solar System: every planet, moon and spacecraft is where it really is right now. Pick something to try.

Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click anything to fly there. The bar at the bottom speeds time up or jumps to a date. Press ? to see this again.

Planet textures: Solar System Scope (CC BY 4.0), NASA data
Oort cloud (faint dots): an illustrative statistical model — no Oort-cloud object has ever been observed there.
